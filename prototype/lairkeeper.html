    Controls

    1. Click one of your units. Blue tiles show where it can move.
    2. Click a blue tile to move, then a hero ringed in red to attack.
    3. Drag the map or use WASD to look around. Scroll to zoom.
    4. Walls and furniture block movement and arrows.
    5. Hover a hero to see where it can strike next turn. E ends your turn.
